Desktop companion sprites
=========================

One directory per character, one file per animation: <character>/<animation>.png.
Each animation has 4 frames of 64x64 on a transparent background (one 256x64 sheet),
played at 0.5 s per frame. Which animation expresses what is defined in
../characters.js; this directory holds images only.

Alignment rules (break them and playback jitters):
- The figure is centered in its cell by the center of its lower body.
- Feet sit on the same row across one character's animations (MIKU y=60, Zhuang y=63).
- Alpha is binary (0 or 255); pixel art has no anti-aliased edges.
